# YOASOBI Song Quiz · 曲当てクイズ · 猜歌測驗

**Play: https://fuyo1622.github.io/yoasobi-song-quiz/**

Guess YOASOBI songs from short clips of their official music videos: from the chorus, from
anywhere in the song, or from everywhere except the chorus. Play the **quiz** (four choices,
a thinking timer and a score) or **practice** (no choices, no score). In English, 繁體中文 or
日本語. All 35 Japanese-language songs are included.

A clip starts at the wrong time, or isn't the chorus? Press **Report chorus time** after the
answer. You can also [open an issue](https://github.com/fuyo1622/yoasobi-song-quiz/issues/new/choose).

This repository only holds the published website and is updated automatically. A fan-made
quiz, not affiliated with YOASOBI or their labels. The videos play in YouTube's embedded player.
